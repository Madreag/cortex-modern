SUMMARY: the referee settles a split identically wherever every half asks the same key and gets an answer, but four shapes break the requirement. (a) A decision outlives its split: a split that heals before any hold is certified writes no frame-group change, so the epoch keeps its first winner and every later tie in it reuses that answer without asking (#4, #5). (b) The client cannot ask inside the bound: routine check-ins every 5.1 s keep the 12-per-60.1 s seat allowance full for about 4.0 s of every 5.1 s (#6), and the exchange has a 250 ms timeout on a fresh TLS connection every time (#7). (c) A kick by a half that has no authority turns 4 retained seats into 3, and that half becomes a majority without the referee (#15). (d) With 3 retained seats and the host held, no rule names a winner (#19).
