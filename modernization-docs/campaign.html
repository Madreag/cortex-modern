Flagship

The Campaign

Cortex Command's built-in strategic campaign — "Conquest" / the Metagame — is officially abandoned. This page is the case for making a real campaign cortex-modern's flagship feature: the one big, identity-defining thing the fork exists to build.

The situation — upstream said it themselves

Open Conquest mode in the shipping game and a notice appears (the text is hard-coded in Source/Menus/MainMenuGUI.cpp, function ShowMetaGameNoticeScreen()):

“Please note that Conquest mode is very INCOMPLETE and flawed, and the Cortex Command Community Project team will be remaking it from the ground-up in future. For now though, it's fully playable… Alternatively, check out Void Wanderers on cccp.mod.io!”

So the official campaign is, by the team's own admission in the shipping game, unfinished — and it redirects players to a community mod.

What the code shows

A direct check of the source and git history (2026-05-16):

  • The Metagame is present and compiled — MetaMan, MetaSave, MetaPlayer, MetagameGUI, plus Data/Base.rte/Activities/MetaFight.lua (~68 KB). Conquest runs; it is "fully playable" as the notice says.
  • But its git history since 2024 is maintenance-only — cppcheck lints, compiler-warning cleanups, incidental timer/save touches. No feature work. No rework branch. None of the upstream WIP branches touch it, and development has been frozen since 2026-04-06.
  • The promised "remaking it from the ground-up" has zero code behind it — it is an aspiration in a UI string, nothing more. Realistically: it is not going to happen upstream.
  • The v6.0 engine overhaul quietly added campaign building blocks — capture points, buy doors, dropship docks, item dispensers, AI tactics — described by the team as "fully available to modders and will be used heavily in our next release." That release never shipped. The primitives exist; nothing assembles them.
  • Minor tell: the same notice offers Conquest "with the A.I. and/or up to three friends" — a stale reference, since multiplayer has since been removed (see ADR-011).

Void Wanderers — the de-facto campaign

Void Wanderers (originally by Weegee, kept alive by community maintainers) is the campaign the base game points you to. It is a total-conversion mod — an FTL / X-COM / Firefly-style space roguelite: captain a ship, run faction missions, manage crew and upgrades. It is widely called "better than the main campaign," and it is unambiguously the community's real meta layer.

It sets the bar — but it also shows the risk: Void Wanderers is a heavily-scripted mod tightly coupled to engine quirks, and its bug threads are full of engine-edge problems (AI uselessly shooting doors it can't breach, miner-dropships that never deploy, version-mismatch crashes). cortex-modern should design its own campaign fresh — from first principles and the Corefall Ideas — not copy Void Wanderers. Learn from it; don't fork it.

Why this is the flagship

The opportunity

It is an officially-sanctioned gap. Upstream admitted the campaign is broken, promised a rework, and walked away — for over two years. Building it is not competing with the team; it is doing the work they publicly deferred.

The design already exists. The Corefall Ideas page catalogues a complete campaign-grade design — mission director, branching objective graphs, factions and diplomacy, storytellers, world events, persistent crew — and almost all of it is [MOD]: Lua, GAScripted and GlobalScript, no engine fork.

It addresses the #2 community complaint (after AI), and it gives cortex-modern a clear identity — a reason for the fork to exist beyond engine cleanup.

What it's made of

The campaign is an assembly of pieces already mapped on the Corefall Ideas page. Most are pure mod work; the engine primitives needed already exist.

[MOD]  Mission director + branching objective graph

A typed objective graph with pacing phases and dynamic reinforcement, on top of CC's GAScripted activity contract. The spine of the campaign.

[MOD]  Factions, reputation, diplomacy, territory

A relationship matrix, reputation-gated buy access, faction territory. CC factions are already .rte modules; a GlobalScript layer adds the strategic dimension.

[MOD]  Storyteller / incident pacing

A RimWorld-style incident director scheduling events against an intensity curve, so the campaign feels authored rather than random.

[MOD]  Procedural mission generator

Parameterised mission templates (assault, defend, escort, sabotage, rescue…) that produce authored-feeling missions with per-faction flavour.

[MOD]  Persistent crew — veterans, scars, XP

Soldiers that survive and carry forward across missions, with progression and visible scars. GlobalScript plus the save system. This is what makes a campaign feel like yours.

[MOD]  Wire up the dormant v6.0 primitives

Capture points, buy doors, dropship docks, item dispensers, AI tactics — all added in v6.0 and sitting unused in vanilla. Using them is data and Lua; the hard engine work is already done.

[MOD] [CODE]  Campaign UI — map, briefings, debrief

A strategic map, mission briefings and an after-action debrief. Immediate-mode HUD drawing is [MOD]; a polished retained-widget map screen would want some engine support.

[MOD] [CODE]  Smarter AI underneath it all

A campaign is only as good as its battles, and AI is the #1 community complaint. The AI overhaul is its own committed flagship — a full layered-hybrid rebuild on the AI page (ADR-014) — and the campaign is built on top of it.

Suggested approach — phased

Phase 0 — the AI floor. Squad and tactical AI (T1-06 + the Corefall AI ideas). The campaign cannot be good if the moment-to-moment fights are not; this comes first and stands on its own.

Phase 1 — un-break vanilla Conquest. Wire the dormant v6.0 primitives into the existing Metagame and smooth its worst rough edges. Low-risk, ships visible value fast, and makes the in-game "INCOMPLETE and flawed" notice less true.

Phase 2 — the real campaign. The full GAScripted campaign: mission director, factions, storytellers, persistent crew — the flagship. Built fresh, clean-room, from the Corefall Ideas.

The standing decision is recorded as ADR-012. The design source is the Corefall Ideas board; supporting engine work is tracked on the Roadmap.
