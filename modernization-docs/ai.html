Foundations

Smarter AI

Cortex Command's AI is the single most-cited weakness in the game — and the upstream lead says so himself. It is also the foundation under everything else: the Campaign is only as good as its battles, and co-op Multiplayer runs enemy AI server-side. And it is ~95% Lua — so fixing it is almost pure mod work.

The headline

AI quality is the #1 complaint across Steam reviews, the forums and mod.io — "units stuck in terrain", weak bunker navigation, shallow squad coordination. Upstream has an open issue for it (#208, "improve squad logic"). And the project lead, Causeless, put it bluntly in the dev Discord (2025-08-09): "the AI is such garbage. it's very frustrating. I want to just fucking delete all the path following code."

That is a rare thing: a major subsystem that the people who own it openly consider broken, have sketched a fix for, and have not executed. It is the most clearly un-owned, sanctioned gap in the whole project — and because CC's AI is overwhelmingly Lua, almost all of the fix is [MOD].

What the code shows

  • CC's AI is ~95% Lua — roughly 5,800 lines in Base.rte/AI/*.lua (NativeHumanAI.lua, HumanBehaviors.lua, SharedBehaviors.lua, etc.). The C++ engine only gives an actor a Controller (the AI "presses the same virtual buttons" a human would), an AIMode enum, and an async, jump-aware A* pathfinder. Everything tactical is Lua — which means it is moddable.
  • What is decent: moment-to-moment combat — velocity-led aim, hearing-based perception, basic cover use. A solo enemy soldier fights reasonably.
  • What is weak: squad and strategic AI — squadmates essentially copy the leader's inputs; there is no real role coordination. And path-following — the lead's own assessment is that "there's more issues with path following and AI navigation than in the pathfinding algorithm itself." The A* algorithm is fine; the Lua that consumes the path is the garbage.
  • Known specific failures: actors stuck on single-pixel obstacles; broken stuck-detection; crab and human locomotion are duplicated and drift apart (a unified ALocomotable was designed in the Discord but never built); ladders barely work ("you can't climb them 90% of the time"); actors without a breaching tool freeze at hardened doors (see the door analysis in the project notes).

The key implication: the expensive part — the pathfinding algorithm — is already good (async, multithreaded, jump-aware C++). The broken part is the Lua path-following and behaviour layer, and that is [MOD]. CC AI can be dramatically improved with no engine fork.

Why this is the foundation

AI is not a side feature — it sits under the other two flagship efforts. A campaign is only as compelling as the firefights inside it. Co-op multiplayer runs all the enemy and ally AI on the server, so the AI is a large part of the multiplayer experience. Even the door problem is an AI problem. Fix the AI and every other effort gets better for free. It is the highest-leverage, lowest-risk, most-validated work available — and the design for a much richer AI is already catalogued on the Corefall Ideas page (the AI & Squad cluster).

What it's made of

The building blocks, mostly drawn from the Corefall Ideas AI cluster. Nearly all of it is mod work on Base.rte/AI; only one item needs the engine.

[MOD]  Locomotion & path-following overhaul

Rewrite the Lua that follows a path — the part the lead calls garbage. Fix stuck-detection, unify the duplicated crab/human locomotion behaviours, make ladders and tight terrain reliable. The A* pathfinder underneath is kept as-is. This is the floor everything else stands on.

[MOD]  Squad roles & coordinated tactics

Replace "squadmates copy the leader" with real roles (point / flank / support), coordinated suppression, and bounding movement. This is exactly upstream issue #208 and roadmap item T1-06.

[MOD]  AI archetypes + layered behaviour stack

Distinct archetypes (Rifleman / Sniper / Assault / Engineer / Medic / Spotter) on a reactive → utility → behaviour-tree stack, instead of one generic brain. Pure Lua.

[MOD]  Priority tables & squad doctrines

A per-actor weighted task table and per-squad doctrines (aggressive / defensive / scout) that bias the whole squad's behaviour. Data plus a Lua scorer.

[MOD] [CODE]  Cover-finder & tactical positioning

Score nearby tiles by line-of-fire block to known threats and pick real cover. A Lua cover-finder using the existing ray-casts is moddable; a batched per-tile query for scale would want a bounded engine binding.

[MOD]  BotMemory — threat & pain memory

Per-actor memory of where threats were last seen and where it was hit, so the AI avoids kill zones and remembers danger. Lua tables on actors, fed by the wound callbacks.

[MOD]  Scaled difficulty

Difficulty that genuinely scales reaction time, aim-settle, miss chance and target priority — not just actor HP. The other half of T1-06.

[MOD]  Environmental competence & reason labels

AI that reacts to hazards (avoid fire, take cover from explosives, handle the door problem), and a debug "Why?" key that surfaces each bot's current reason — invaluable for tuning. Pure Lua.

[CODE]  Hierarchical pathfinding

The one engine-side item: CC's pathfinder is flat. A tile / chunk / region hierarchy with dynamic invalidation on terrain change is a bounded C++ change — worthwhile only after the Lua path-following is fixed, since that is where the real pain is.

Suggested approach — phased

Phase 0 — fix the floor. The path-following / stuck-detection / locomotion overhaul, ladders, and door behaviour. Make the existing AI stop embarrassing itself. Pure Lua, immediately visible, and it stands on its own regardless of what comes next.

Phase 1 — squad depth. Real roles, coordination and scaled difficulty (T1-06 / issue #208). The first thing that makes battles feel designed.

Phase 2 — the rich stack. Archetypes, priority tables, doctrines, cover-finder, BotMemory — the AI the campaign and co-op multiplayer actually deserve.

This is the recommended first real work for cortex-modern: highest leverage, lowest risk, almost pure [MOD], and a problem upstream has openly admitted and not fixed. See the Roadmap (T1-06) and the Corefall Ideas AI cluster.
